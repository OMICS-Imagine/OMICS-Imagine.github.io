## About Me

I lead the Multi-Omics Analytics group in the Bioinformatics Hub of [Institut Imagine](https://www.institutimagine.org/en) (INSERM UMR 1163, Paris). I set the strategy for multi-omics data analysis, technology adoption, and pipeline development across research and translational programs.

I am a computational biologist working on high-dimensional sequencing data and reproducible workflows. My work spans genome biology, cancer, human genetics, and immunology. I previously worked as Principal Scientist in Precision Oncology at Sanofi, where I developed spatial biology approaches for research and development, and held academic positions at the University of Oxford (EMBO Long-Term Fellow, Department of Oncology) and Institut Curie (Ph.D. in Genomics).

---

## Research Interests

- **Translational research for rare diseases:** genetic diagnosis, variant interpretation, patient stratification, biomarker discovery, and molecular profiles linked to clinical phenotypes
- **Multi-omics analysis:** integration of genomics, transcriptomics, and epigenomics for research and translational programs
- **Transcriptomics:** bulk, single-cell, and spatial transcriptomics, including spatial multi-omics
- **Genomics and epigenomics:** whole-genome and whole-exome sequencing, DNA methylation, ChIP-seq, CUT&Tag, and ATAC-seq
- **Workflows:** scalable, production-ready pipelines for sequencing data
